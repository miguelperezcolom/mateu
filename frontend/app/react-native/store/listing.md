**Name** (30): `Mateu`

**Subtitle** — App Store (30): `Your business apps, native`

**Promotional text** — App Store (170):
> Open your organisation's Mateu apps on your phone: scan their QR code, sign in, done. Lists, forms, records and dashboards, all native.

**Keywords** — App Store (100, comma-separated, no spaces after commas):
> business,enterprise,forms,crud,backoffice,low-code,qr,thin client,erp,crm,dashboard,open source

**Description** (4000, both stores):
> Mateu brings your organisation's business applications to your phone.
>
> Companies build their internal and customer-facing apps with Mateu, an open-source framework, and run them on their own servers. This app is the client: add an app by scanning its QR code — the "Open on mobile" button of its web version shows one — or by typing its address, and it opens with native lists, forms, records, wizards and dashboards.
>
> • Add apps like you add accounts to an authenticator: scan, confirm the domain, done.
> • Several apps side by side, each with its own sign-in. Switch between them in one tap.
> • Sign in with your organisation's account (single sign-on in the system browser — the app never sees your password).
> • Fast start: an app you have opened before shows its screens right away.
> • Move your list to another phone with one QR code (addresses only, never sign-ins).
> • Light and dark themes, large text and screen readers supported.
>
> Privacy first: the Mateu app collects nothing — no account, no analytics, no tracking. What you enter goes to your organisation's server only.
>
> Want to try it? The list comes with a demo app you can open without an account.
>
> Building apps with Mateu? See https://mateu.io.

**What's new** (first release):
> First release: add Mateu apps by QR code or address, sign in with your organisation, and use them natively.
