# field-decorations

**Pins:** presentation declared on a reflected page and its fields: the class `@Style` /
`@CssClasses` on the page component; `@OptionsLayout(columns)` (`optionsColumns`); a field's
`@Style` (on the field AND its wrapper); `@DivStyle` (the field's `attributes` entry `divStyle`);
and the `@Notice` String-field marker — the field's value as a `${state.<field>}` notice text with
its theme/slim/action. A field's `@CssClasses` reaches no wire member in Java, so it is not
declared here.
