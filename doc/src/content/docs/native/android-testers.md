The Mateu app — any Mateu UI on your phone — is already on the App Store. To be published on **Google Play**, a new app must first
run a **closed test**: at least **12 people** opted in as testers for **14 days in a row**. You can be
one of them. It takes about five minutes, and the app is the same free app everyone will get.

## What you need

- An **Android phone** with the Play Store.
- The **Google account** that phone uses for Play. The tester list goes by Google account, so join
  with the same one. If you are not sure, open the Play Store and tap your profile picture.

## Become a tester

1. **Join the testers group.** Open
   [groups.google.com/g/mateu-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/mateu-testers) and click
   **Join group**, signed in with that Google account.
   Joining is what puts you on the tester list.
2. **Accept the invitation.** Open
   [play.google.com/apps/testing/io.mateu.app](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/io.mateu.app),
   on the phone or on a computer signed in with the same account, and tap **Become a tester**.
   If the page says the app is not available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and reload it.
3. **Install the app from Play.** On the phone, open
   [the Mateu app on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.mateu.app) and
   tap **Install**. The listing is only visible to testers, so it must be the account from step 2.
4. **Keep it installed for 14 days.** The 14 days count only while at least 12 testers stay opted in,
   so please do not uninstall it or leave the test before then. Opening it now and then helps too.

## Something to try

The app opens on your own list of UIs: empty at first. Tap **Add a UI** › **Type an address instead**
and type one of these demos:

- 🏛 `museums.ec1.mateu.io` — the collections of three museums: artists, works, exhibitions, loans.
- 🚀 `space.ec1.mateu.io` — a space-themed demo.

Or scan a Mateu QR code, such as the one the Mateu IDE plugins show for an app running on your
computer ([Open on mobile](/ux-patterns/mobile-app/)). The app asks for no sign-up and no personal data.

## Tell us what you find

Anything that looks wrong, is confusing or crashes: open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/miguelperezcolom/mateu/issues) with your phone model and Android version,
and a screenshot if you can. You can also leave private feedback for the developers from the app's
Play listing.

## Leaving the test

Once the app is public you can stay: you will receive the public updates like anyone else. To leave
earlier, open the [testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/io.mateu.app) again and tap
**Leave the program**. You can then keep the app installed or uninstall it.

## Your data

Joining the group and the test shares your Google account's email with the app's developer, as Google
Play does for every closed test. The app itself works like the published
one: what it stores is the list of UIs you add, on your phone.
